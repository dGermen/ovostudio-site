        Process

        1. 01

          Brief

          You describe the product, the constraints and how it should be made. I send back what I see as the risks.

        2. 02

          Concept

          Two or three directions as quick 3D studies, so we are choosing between real shapes, not words.

        3. 03

          CAD & DFM

          The chosen direction becomes production CAD, detailed for the process it will be made with.

        4. 04

          Handover

          Files, drawings and renders, plus a short note on what a manufacturer needs to know.

        Studio

        Ovo Studio is a one-person industrial design studio. I design consumer products and enclosures, and I detail them for the process that will actually make them: injection moulding, sheet metal or 3D printing.

        I work in Rhino and render in Blender. Every image on this site comes from a real CAD model, so the geometry behind a picture is the geometry you get.

        Based in Türkiye, working with clients in Europe and the US
