Skincare set, render pack

Five products, one lighting setup, marketplace-ready

  • 2026
  • 3D modelling & rendering

A five-product skincare line modelled specifically to be photographed: real glass wall thickness, ribbed caps that catch a highlight, and clean cylindrical bodies that a label can be wrapped onto. Shot as a family group, as a marketplace main image on pure white, and as a macro.

Why this exists

Most product renders fail in the same place: the glass. Modelled as a single surface with no thickness, a bottle refracts like a soap bubble and the eye rejects it instantly. This set was built to show what changes when the geometry is honest.

Glass that behaves like glass

Every body is a revolved outer profile minus a revolved cavity: 3.5 mm walls on the serum bottle, 4 mm on the pump bottle, and a 10 mm solid glass base on the jar, which is what produces that heavy lens at the bottom. The dropper pipette sits inside the serum bottle, so it distorts through the wall exactly as it would on a shelf.

Built for listings, not just for a portfolio

The main image is rendered on pure white with the product filling about 85% of the frame and grounded by its own shadow, which is what marketplaces ask for. The bodies are clean surfaces of revolution, so artwork maps with a simple cylindrical projection: a new label is a new render, not a new model.

What you receive

4K images for each angle, the marketplace main image on pure white, colour and material variants rendered from the same model, and STEP and 3DM if the parts ever need to be made.

Deliverables
