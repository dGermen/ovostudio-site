Drift portable speaker

An enclosure designed around its parting line

  • 2026
  • Product design & DFM
  • Concept project

A 186 mm portable speaker detailed for injection moulding: two housings that meet on a 0.2 mm shut line, 2.2 mm walls, 1.5° of draft off the parting plane, and a perforated grille worked into the front face.

Size186 × 70 × 62 mm
Wall thickness2.2 mm
Draft1.5° off the parting plane
Shut line0.2 mm
Parts16
HousingsABS, approx. 91 g
Drivers2 × 52 mm
Grille103 holes, Ø3.6 mm, 6 mm pitch
FilesSTEP · 3DM · OBJ · PDF

The brief

A self-set brief: a rugged portable speaker a small brand could realistically tool up. That means two moulded housings, no undercuts anywhere, soft overmoulded caps to survive being dropped, and a shape that still looks designed rather than engineered.

Designing around the mould, not after it

The widest cross-section of the body sits exactly on the parting plane, so the mould opens cleanly in two directions and the split line lands where the eye expects a seam. Every wall tapers 1.5° away from that plane, the two housings meet on a deliberate 0.2 mm shut line, and the soft end caps wrap the seam where hands and drops land.

Acoustics and the front face

Two 52 mm drivers sit on an internal baffle, with a passive radiator at the back and a bass port through the rear housing. The front face is perforated with 103 holes of 3.6 mm on a staggered 6 mm pitch: open enough for the drivers, tight enough to read as one surface from a metre away.

What a manufacturer receives

Production CAD as STEP and 3DM, dimensioned drawings, an exploded view and a bill of materials for all 16 parts, plus notes on wall thickness, draft, the parting line and gate positions. Everything shown here comes from that same model.

Deliverables

  • Production CAD (STEP, 3DM)
  • Dimensioned PDF drawings
  • Exploded view and 16-part bill of materials
  • Photoreal renders, 4K
